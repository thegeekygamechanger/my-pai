My PAI is a free, open-source AI app. Add your own API key and chat, or hand over a piece of work and let it get on with it. There are no ads, no subscription and no account: your key talks to the AI service directly.

<b>Your own keys</b>
Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek and other OpenAI-compatible services. On Auto, each message goes to the model that suits it: the quick one for small questions, the strongest for hard ones.

<b>Chat</b>
Clear answers with web search, images and PDFs. You see the answer, not a wall of steps.

<b>Work</b>
Give it a task and it goes step by step with files in a private workspace, the web, and your GitHub and Gmail if you connect them. With a Claude key it can also make and convert files (a PDF from your notes, a spreadsheet, a chart or a diagram) and save them to your workspace. It always asks before it sends an email or changes anything.

<b>Everything else</b>
• Scheduled tasks that run by themselves and notify you
• Pictures on request, with your own Gemini, OpenAI or Pollinations key
• Voice typing
• End-to-end encrypted sync with the Windows and Linux app, paired by QR code, through a relay that stores nothing
• Share a single chat with a four-character code
• App lock, light and dark themes

<b>Private by design</b>
Chats, files and keys stay on your device, encrypted. Nothing is collected and there is no tracking.

Source code, the computer app and the relay are at https://github.com/thegeekygamechanger/my-pai (MIT licence).

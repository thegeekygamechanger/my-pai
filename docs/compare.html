How My PAI compares

My PAI is one of several apps with public source code that let you use AI with your own keys. They are good projects, and the right one depends on what you need. This page sets out plain facts side by side so you can choose. It is not a ranking.

Where My PAI is different

  • No account and no server to set up. Install it, add a key (or point it at Ollama) and start. There is nothing to sign in to.
  • Phone and computer in step. Chats, files and keys sync between your own devices, encrypted end to end, linked by a code or a QR picture. The relay in between stores nothing.
  • It asks first. Anything that changes a file, sends an email or calls an MCP server is shown to you for a yes or no.
  • Small and plain. One MIT-licensed codebase for Android, Windows and Linux, with no paid plan.

Where the others go further

  • Apple devices. My PAI has no macOS or iPhone app. Several of the apps above do.
  • Teams. My PAI is for one person. Open WebUI, LibreChat and AnythingLLM can be hosted for many users.
  • A browser version. My PAI is an installed app only.
  • Years of features. These projects are older and larger, with plug-ins, marketplaces and document tools My PAI does not have.

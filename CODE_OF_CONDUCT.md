# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in My PAI to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

What helps:

- Being kind and patient, and assuming the other person means well
- Respecting views and experience that differ from yours
- Giving criticism that is about the work, and taking it gracefully
- Owning a mistake, saying sorry and learning from it

What is not acceptable:

- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind
- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling
- Harassment, in public or in private
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or email, without their clear permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be thought out of place in a professional setting

## Where it applies

In every space of this project: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere a person is speaking for the project.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer privately. Either use the form in the app (Settings, Send feedback), or use GitHub's own report link on the comment or profile concerned ("Report content"). Every report is read, and who made it is kept private.

## What happens next

The maintainer may remove or edit comments, commits, issues and other contributions that go against this Code of Conduct, and may ban someone, for a time or for good, for behaviour that is threatening, offensive or harmful.

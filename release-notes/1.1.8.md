- **Your chats are in the side menu.** Under Scheduled tasks the menu now lists every chat, newest first, and scrolls. Long-press one (right-click on a computer) for a rounded menu: pin, rename, share with a code, delete. The search button beside "History" opens the full list with search. On a computer the open side panel shows the same menu; tap the mark to fold it away.
- **Share from the top of any chat.** The three dots are gone. Beside the + there is a share button that gives a four-character code for the whole conversation. Tap or long-press the chat's name for the model and tools, as before.
- **The model under the name.** The line under the chat's name shows just the model that is answering, and it is no longer repeated in the corner under each reply.
- **A smaller Chat / Work switch, and a message box that invites you.** The empty box shows a short line that keeps changing instead of "Message".
- **Replies spread in.** Words arrive behind a wide, soft edge instead of a hard one, and each new part of a reply (a step, a table, a saved file) opens out as it fades in.
- Asked who developed it, the assistant says who made the app and which AI model is answering.
- **Send feedback from Settings.** A short form slides up: write what works and what does not, add your email if you would like a reply, and send. It carries only that text, the email and the app version.

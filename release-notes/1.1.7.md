- **Work makes and converts files.** Ask Work (with a Claude key) to turn your notes into a PDF, build a Word or Excel file, or draw a chart or a diagram. It does the job in Claude's code sandbox, taking files from your workspace as they are when they are not plain text (a Word or Excel file, a picture, a PDF), and what it makes is saved into your workspace and shown in the chat with Open and Folder buttons; pictures appear right in the conversation. The sandbox is always available in Work, so there is no switch to remember.
- **It does the work, and says so plainly when it cannot.** The assistant is told to use its tools instead of describing how you could do the job yourself, and never to say a file was made or a message sent unless that really happened.
- **Replies are written out, never dropped in.** In Chat and in Work, a reply is written at an even pace with the last words fading in, whether it arrived word by word or all at once after a step of work. Lists, tables and code grow the same way.
- **History slides up.** History opens as a sheet over the chat you are in, on phones and computers, instead of replacing the screen.
- **Pictures and videos only with a key that can make them.** The free picture service is gone. With a Gemini, OpenAI or Pollinations key the feature is there; without one it is not shown anywhere, Settings included.

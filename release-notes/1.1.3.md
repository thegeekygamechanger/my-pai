- **Pictures for everyone.** Ask for a picture in any chat and it is drawn even if you only have a Claude key: with no Gemini or OpenAI key, the app uses Pollinations, a free open-source service (one picture every 45 seconds or so; switch it off under Settings, Pictures and videos). Videos need a key: Gemini, or Pollinations, which runs the open-source Wan model.
- **Notifications.** When a reply is ready while you are in another app, the phone and the computer tell you. A scheduled task announces its result at its time with a sound. Both can be switched off under Settings, Notifications.
- **Settings has a new layout**: labelled groups of rows, each opening a page of its own, with the theme switch right in the list.
- **Updates are found by themselves.** The app now looks for a new version when it opens and every half hour while it stays open, and announces it at the top of the chat screen: one tap installs it. Before this, computers only found an update when you pressed "Check for updates", and phones looked once a day at most.
- A new version is only offered once the file for your system is really there, so Windows no longer gets an update that cannot be downloaded yet.

If you are on 1.1.2 or earlier, press **Settings, App update, Check for updates** once to get this version; after that it is automatic.

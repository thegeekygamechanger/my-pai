- **Auto model.** New chats use Auto: each message goes to the model that suits it, the quick one for small talk and simple facts, the everyday one for ordinary questions, the strongest for long, technical or multi-step requests and for Work. Pick a model yourself at any time under the chat's three-dot menu or in Settings.
- The colours behind a chat stay clearly visible, and keep moving, after the conversation has started, in dark mode too.

- **Quicker, straighter answers.** A vague or everyday question no longer sets off a long chain of searches: a chat now searches a few times at most, answers from what it found, says what it assumed, and says so plainly when something could not be confirmed instead of guessing. It also knows today's date.
- **New app icon**, with the app's own colours.
- **Opening a shared chat is more forgiving.** Type or paste the four-character code and send it: the app recognises it either way, and tells you plainly if nothing is being shared under it. A share now stays up for its ten minutes even after its window is closed, so you can go and send the code.
- On a computer, tap the app's mark at the top of the side menu to fold the menu away and open it again.
- **GitHub and Gmail in every chat.** Connected accounts now work in any chat, new or already under way, in Chat and in Work. Typing `@github`, `@gmail` or `@mail` is enough; you no longer have to pick it from the list.

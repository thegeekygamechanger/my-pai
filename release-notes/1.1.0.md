- **Pictures and videos.** Ask for an image or a short video in any chat and it appears in the conversation. Pictures use your Gemini or OpenAI key; videos use your Gemini key.
- **A calmer chat.** Searches, pages read, code and reasoning no longer fill the screen: while a reply is on its way you see one animated line, then the answer. "Show the working" in the chat menu brings the detail back.
- **Share with four characters.** Sharing a chat gives a four-character code, already copied. Paste it into the message box on another device and the app asks before opening the chat as a new one of your own. A code works for ten minutes, and each hand-over is encrypted with a key made just for it.
- **Keyboard and mouse.** On a computer, Enter sends and Shift+Enter starts a new line; right-click a chat in History, or one of your messages, for the same options a long press gives on a phone.
- **Voice typing on computers without a key.** With no Groq or OpenAI key, speech is recognised on the computer itself after a small language pack is fetched once (English, Hindi, Telugu, Gujarati and eight more).
- **Edit and resend.** Every message you sent has Copy and Edit under it; edit, press send, and the reply is written again.
- Type `@` in the message box to bring in a connected GitHub or Gmail account, and pick the repositories it should work with.
- Web search is on for every chat and piece of work unless you turn it off.
- Scheduled tasks come with suggestions you can add with one tap.
- While a reply is being written you can scroll up and read; a small button brings you back down.
- The weather gradient now sits behind chats, the side menu and scheduled tasks, and moves while the app is working.
- The model and tools are under the three-dot menu of the chat; the suggested prompts on the first screen can be tapped.
- "Agent" is now called "Work". Chat or Work is chosen before the first message; after that the switch stays put and offers a fresh start instead.
